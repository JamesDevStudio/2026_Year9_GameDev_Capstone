Requirements & Scope
==================================================

EXAMPLE GAME: Reef Rescue
GENRE: 2D top-down collection game
TARGET AUDIENCE: Year 7 science students

FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
------------------------------
FR1. Move with WASD
FR2. Boost with the space bar
FR3. Score tracker for progress
FR4. Rubbish floating in water that can damage you
FR5. Health bar to track health points

NON-FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
------------------------------
NFR1. No gamebreaking bugs
NFR2. Available on unity 2D
NFR3. Quick tutorial to get beginners comfortable with the controls
NFR4. Clean UI with no "clutter"

CONSTRAINTS
------------------------------
CON1. Four weeks to complete
CON2. All images, sounds and fonts must be royalty-free or student-created
CON3. It must run on school Windows laptops using approved software
CON4. The game must be suitable for Year 7 students

SCOPE — IN SCOPE
------------------------------
✓ One reef themed level
✓ Submarine and hazards have sprites
✓ Tutorial

SCOPE — OUT OF SCOPE
------------------------------
✗ Online leaderboards
✗ Online Multiplayer
✗ Custom music
